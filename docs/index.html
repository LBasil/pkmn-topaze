Pokémon Topaze

A Pokémon FireRed ROM hack set in Kanto, twenty years after the original adventure. Ten years ago a mysterious aura swept the region and mutated the types of every Pokémon. Now a new team, Team Onybris, is chasing Jirachi's wish.

Status: in development (pre-alpha). The data on these pages is generated straight from the game's source code, so it is always what the ROM actually contains. Story scripts, new maps and the Pokémon League are still being built.

What's different

  • New types for all 151 Pokémon (66 changed) and a brand new Fairy type.
  • Redesigned starters: Bulbasaur Grass/Ground, Charmander Fire/Ice, Squirtle Water/Steel.
  • Eight gym champions with full 4–5 Pokémon teams and custom movesets.
  • A harder world: trainers are about 12% higher level and wild Pokémon 2–3 levels higher.
  • Quality of life: TMs are infinite, HMs can be forgotten and field moves (Cut, Surf, Strength…) no longer need a Pokémon that knows them; built-in Exp. Share, run anywhere, fast text. Cut / Strength / Rock Smash / Flash are real attacks.
  • Gen 4 physical/special split: each move is physical or special on its own.
  • Rebalanced moves: more reliable big attacks, buffed weak ones.
  • The hero's hometown is now Grenalux; your rival is Zephyr by default.
