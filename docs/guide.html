Player guide

Playing the game

  1. Get the Topaze ROM (pokefirered.gba). It is built from the source code, see the technical docs; no original ROM is needed.
  2. Open it in an emulator such as mGBA (PC/Mac/Linux), or on a flash cart / handheld that runs GBA games.
  3. Saves are standard GBA saves. Use fresh saves: a save from another hack or from vanilla FireRed will not match Topaze's data.

New rules to know

Types changed

Don't trust your memory: Persian is Dark, Gengar is Ghost/Dark, Blastoise is Water/Steel, Gyarados is Water/Dragon, Dragonite is Dragon/Flying, Kangaskhan is Normal/Fighting… Always check the Pokédex before a fight.

The Fairy type

Fairy is strong against Fighting, Dragon and Dark; weak (not very effective) against Fire, Poison and Steel; and Dragon moves don't affect it. Poison and Steel moves hit Fairy for double damage. Hidden Power can never be Fairy. Covet, Hyper Voice and Sweet Kiss are now Fairy moves.

Physical / special

Topaze keeps the Generation III rule: a move's category depends on its type, not on the move. Fairy counts as special.

Field moves and TMs

  • TMs are infinite: teaching one no longer uses it up.
  • No need to learn HMs for the overworld: Cut, Rock Smash, Strength, Waterfall and Surf work as soon as you have the matching badge, with any Pokémon in your party. (Fly, Teleport and Flash still need a Pokémon that knows them.)
  • HMs can be forgotten whenever you like, no Move Deleter required.
  • Cut is Grass-type (60 power), Strength is Rock-type, Rock Smash has 60 power, and Flash is a weak Electric attack. They still open the same paths in the overworld.

Difficulty

Trainers are roughly 12% higher level than in FireRed (at least +1), and wild Pokémon in tall grass are 2–3 levels higher. Gym champions use proper 4–5 Pokémon teams with moves matched to their type theme. Raise your levels, and teach every Pokémon moves of its own types: about 130 extra moves were added so Pokémon can learn attacks of their new types.

Quality of life

  • Built-in Exp. Share: every living Pokémon in your party earns experience after a battle.
  • Run everywhere, including inside buildings and caves.
  • Fast text by default (you can change it in Options).
  • Infinite TMs and forgettable HMs (see above).

The eight champions

You'll meet them in this order: Kay (Fire), Sylvestre (Bug/Grass), Hera (Dragon/Flying), Grim (Ice), Achlys (Poison), Nox (Dark), Eddie (Electric) and Hepha (Steel). Full teams are on the Gym Champions page.

The champions currently reuse the sprites of the original gym leaders; custom sprites and the final League are planned.

Known limitations

  • This build is untested in-game on many points. Please report crashes, text overflowing a box, or anything that looks off.
  • Text boxes may overflow slightly where "ROCKET" became the longer "ONYBRIS".
  • The game text is in English only.
